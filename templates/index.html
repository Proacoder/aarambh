About Us

We believe a career decision deserves more than a guess.

Career Verse started as an open-source idea: career guidance shouldn't live in scattered PDFs, expensive counsellors, or one-size-fits-all quizzes. It should be a living, visual map — something you can return to, adjust and grow with.

The project is built by developers and educators who wanted a tool that respects a learner's time: clear steps, honest information, and no noise. Everything here is free to explore, and the code behind it is open for anyone to inspect, use, or improve.

Our Roots

Every path forward stands on a circle of people.

Long before roadmaps and dashboards, communities mapped their world by hand — figures dancing in a circle around a shared tree, each one holding the next. That's the spirit Career Verse borrows: no one figures out a career alone, and every stage of growth is drawn from the ones who came before.

This is why the project stays open-source and free — a modern circle, still holding hands.

Manual

Set it up, understand it, run it yourself.

Career Verse is open source under the AARAMBH project. Below is everything you need to get a local copy running, plus where to go for help.

Get the source

Clone it, read through the setup notes, or open an issue.

The full codebase lives on GitHub. It's free to use, easy to fork, and open to contributions from anyone who wants to improve it.
